What am I looking at?

Each line is one probe. It shows the tiny voltage between that probe and the reference pin in the soil — usually less than a thousandth of a volt.

For the first half a minute or so the lines drift while the probes settle in. After that, a line that drifts or bumps may be the mushroom. A sudden jump on every line at once is more likely the wires being knocked.

A red notch means that probe did something unusual compared with its own last few minutes. There is no "right" shape. Watch for patterns that repeat, and for what happens after you try something.
