About

Listening to fungi

Mushrooms and the networks they grow from make tiny, slow electrical changes. Researchers at the University of Queensland study these signals to learn what they might mean.

MycoKit lets anyone record them, with a phone and no programming. Each recording you share adds to a growing collection that the project can compare.

Why turn it into sound?

Signals that change over hours are hard to take in by eye. Played back as sound, a whole day fits into half a minute, and patterns that repeat are easier to notice. The sound always comes with the picture — it helps you follow the trace, it doesn't replace it.

What it can't tell you

Nobody yet knows what most of these signals mean, or whether a change is the mushroom "responding". MycoKit shows you what was measured and when. What it means is still an open question — which is why your recordings are useful.
