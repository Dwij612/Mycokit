The Underground Brain Project

Part of the Underground Brain Project at the University of Queensland, which explores the electromagnetic communication systems of fungal mycelium.

MycoKit records the tiny electrical signals of a mushroom with four thin probes and a phone. This is where recordings are shared: the signal, the photos and what people noticed.

How it works

  1. Set up the kit. Probes into the mushroom, reference pin in the soil. The steps
  2. Record. Join the kit's Wi-Fi with the QR code on the card and press Start. The kit has no internet; it doesn't need any.
  3. Share it here. When you're done, the kit saves one file to your phone, with your photos inside. Back on the internet, upload it using the access code printed on the kit.
