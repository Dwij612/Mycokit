The Underground Brain Project

UQ MycoWave is part of the Underground Brain Project at the University of Queensland, which explores the electromagnetic communication systems of fungal mycelium. For more background on the research aims and team, see the project's page at UQ.

The studies here are recordings made with MycoKit, on cultivated lab specimens and in the field, together with photos of the mushroom and notes on what was observed.

On iNaturalist

The project's iNaturalist page collects observations of fungi that are linked to our bioelectricity recording work. The team chooses which studies from this site go there; you don't post there yourself.

Observations can be contributed by:

  • members of the Underground Brain Project research group,
  • students and staff taking part in related courses or research projects, and
  • invited collaborators and citizen scientists working with us on fungal bioelectricity.

Interested in contributing observations or collaborating on fungal bioelectricity recordings? Contact the project admins (mycowave) through iNaturalist messages.

Privacy

Nothing is uploaded. This website is part of a thesis on interaction design and citizen science. You can go through every step of sharing a recording, but the upload is simulated: your recording, notes and photos never leave your phone, and no server keeps them.

What a shared study holds: the recording (the four probes, one reading a second), the recording ID, the date, the name you chose to show (if any), your notes and your photos.

Photos are taken in the kit app and come inside the bundle, with when and where each was taken. In this prototype the location is simulated: the kit app runs on the kit's own Wi-Fi, where phones don't share their location. The study page shows the location and links to it on a map.

Who can see it: only you, in this browser on this phone. The example studies are the thesis's own recordings.

For how long: until you clear this browser's data, or use Clear what I've shared below.

Removing a study

Email the project team with your study's recording ID (it looks like FK-7Q4M) and we'll remove it — photos, notes and data. In this simulation your study is only on your phone, so you can also clear it yourself.
